## Purpose

Ensure buyers receive an evidence-backed compatibility audit and implementation scope instead of an unbounded Siri promise.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Fixed-scope audit
The service SHALL deliver a per-target evidence report, feasible journey map, risk list and bounded implementation plan for one repository and up to three journeys.

#### Scenario: Completed audit
- **WHEN** an audit is delivered
- **THEN** every conclusion is classified by evidence and platform

### Requirement: Claim provenance
The service SHALL market a capability only when its named platform and journey have passed the pilot evidence ladder.

#### Scenario: Landing-page claim review
- **WHEN** a claim names Siri or Apple Intelligence support
- **THEN** it references the supported domain, platform and proof artifact
